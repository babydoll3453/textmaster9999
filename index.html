About TextMaster

Simple tools. Useful results. Your text stays yours.

Built for everyday work

TextMaster is a free browser-based toolkit by Cartoon.inc. Use it for quick writing, formatting, cleaning and organizing tasks on desktop or mobile.

Privacy comes first

These tools process text locally in your browser. Your text is not sent to a TextMaster server. Copy and download actions happen on your device.

Privacy note: TextMaster does not currently have an account system or cloud storage. Avoid closing the page before copying or downloading any result you want to keep.
