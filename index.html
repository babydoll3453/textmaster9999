About TextMaster

Useful tools without unnecessary complexity.

Built for everyday writing

TextMaster is a free browser-based toolkit created by Cartoon.inc. Use it for writing, studying, content preparation and everyday text tasks. Your text is processed locally in your browser; it is not uploaded to a server. No account is required.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using TextMaster.

Is TextMaster free?Yes. All tools on this page are free to use.
Do I need an account?No login or account is required.
Is my text uploaded?No. The tools run in your browser. Avoid entering sensitive information on shared devices.
Can I use it on mobile?Yes. It is designed for phones, tablets and computers.
